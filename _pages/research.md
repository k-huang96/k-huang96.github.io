My research focuses on methodological and theoretical statistics for **heterogeneous and structured data**, especially when observations exhibit spatial, temporal, functional, or network dependence. I develop models that are both statistically principled and computationally practical.

## Bayesian spatial learning and clustering

I study graph-informed Bayesian random partition models for spatial domain identification and heterogeneous spatial data. My work investigates posterior consistency, adaptive estimation, and computationally efficient partition inference using predictive spanning trees and graph-based representations.

**Related work:** [*Consistent Bayesian Spatial Domain Partitioning Using Predictive Spanning Tree Methods*](https://doi.org/10.1214/26-AOS2651), *Annals of Statistics* (2026).

## High-dimensional statistics and spatial omics

Spatial transcriptomics and other spatial multimodal data combine thousands of molecular features with spatial dependence. I develop Bayesian methods that jointly learn spatial domains and locally relevant features, together with scalable posterior computation and theoretical guarantees.

**Related work:** [*Consistent Bayesian Local Spatial Feature Selection with Application to Spatial Multimodal Omics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30658), manuscript under major revision at *JASA*.

## Spatial causal inference and point processes

I am interested in causal inference with spatial spillover and interference, particularly for environmental and policy questions. My projects also use spatio-temporal point processes to understand mobility and event data, including wildfire-related air pollution and transportation systems.

## Functional and spatio-temporal data analysis

My earlier work includes inference for dependent functional observations, EEG and event-related potential signals, and models for spatially and temporally indexed point processes. These projects connect statistical theory with applications in neuroscience, transportation, and other scientific fields.

## Emerging interests

I am exploring statistical learning for richer structured objects and new AI-enabled systems, including heterogeneity in transportation and the intersection of spatial reasoning and embodied AI.
